FAQ

these are the questions that get asked over and over. just click one to see the answer.

check the schedule page or just look at the board in the gym. the website schedule is still incomplete so the board is better right now.

no hands (unless youre the goalie). throw in when it goes out on the side. dont push or trip people. mr higgins will tell you if theres any special rules for the game that day.

practice small touches with the ball. pass back and forth with someone. try not to just boot it as hard as you can every time. control is more important than power most of the time.

athletic shoes, clothes you can move in, and a water bottle. put them out the night before so you dont forget.

mostly just showing up ready and trying. participation and effort. if you sit out or dont try it hurts your grade.

tell mr higgins at the start of class. you still have to participate but you might lose some points. try not to make it a regular thing.

more questions i should probably add later:
- what if im absent?
- what happens if it rains?
- can i borrow stuff?

still confused? just ask mr higgins before or after class.
