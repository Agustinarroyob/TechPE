THE EVERY-CLASS CHECKLIST

PE class expectations

A good class starts with being ready, being safe, and giving things a try. Here’s the stuff I explain all the time.

Bring this stuff

  • Athletic shoes that stay on your feet and work for moving around.
  • PE clothes you can move in. Change when class expectations ask you to.
  • A water bottle so you can get a drink when we have a break.

Every class, please

Be ready

Get changed on time, listen for directions, and have your gear with you.

Be safe and respectful

Use equipment the right way, keep hands and feet to yourself, and let everyone join in.

Give honest effort

You don’t have to be the best player. Try the activity, practice, and keep improving.

Listen and reset

Stop when the signal is given, listen to the next directions, and help put equipment away.

How grading works

Most of your grade is participation and effort: showing up prepared, joining activities, following directions, and trying to improve. Exact grading details can depend on the unit, so check with Mr. Higgins if you’re unsure.

Prep tip: Put your PE clothes and shoes by your backpack the night before. Don’t make forgetting clothes a habit!
